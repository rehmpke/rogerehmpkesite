Institutional Accessibility Program Mobilization

Rapid development of an institution-wide accessibility governance program aligned with DOJ Title II requirements, establishing sustainable workflows, risk-based prioritization, and system-level improvements.

Kankakee Community College
June 2025 - April 2026

Overview

Led the rapid mobilization of an institutional accessibility program to meet federal compliance requirements under a fixed deadline. Established governance structures, intake workflows, and a risk-based remediation model across a decentralized web ecosystem.

Key Focus Areas

Governance
Established cross-divisional coordination, executive alignment, and a defensible compliance posture.
Risk-Based Prioritization
Implemented a risk × usage model to focus remediation efforts on high-impact content and systems.
System Design
Improved CMS templates, forms, and content structures to reduce future accessibility debt.
Institutional Adoption
Built training programs, intake workflows, and vendor governance to support long-term sustainability.

This work established a sustainable, institution-wide approach to accessibility grounded in governance, prioritization, and continuous improvement rather than one-time remediation.
