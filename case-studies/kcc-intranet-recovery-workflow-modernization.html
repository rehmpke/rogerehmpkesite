MyKCC Portal Modernization and Digital Workplace Transformation

Helped lead the replacement of KCC’s legacy SharePoint 2013 portal with Ellucian Experience, SharePoint Online, and connected publishing workflows. The project preserved institutional content, clarified ownership, and established an employee information and communications model still in use years after launch.

The problem

The legacy MyKCC portal combined SharePoint 2013 and Ellucian functionality, providing students, faculty, and staff with applications, departmental resources, team sites, communications, and campus services.

The platform’s end-of-life created a hard deadline to preserve critical employee resources, departmental information, communications, and operational content.

There was no direct migration path. Governance and publishing workflows needed to change, content was spread across repositories, and students and employees needed different experiences.

The work required decisions about what to preserve, where it belonged, and who would maintain it.

My role

As Coordinator of Web Content and Design, I led work on information architecture, governance, content review, stakeholder coordination, communications, training, and implementation.

I took primary responsibility for researching, designing, documenting, implementing, and supporting much of the SharePoint Online environment, adapting Microsoft implementation patterns to institutional needs.

I built most of the information sites, lists, libraries, navigation, and governance components. I also migrated and reconstructed content, working with departments to decide what to preserve and establish future ownership.

Working with ITS, Marketing, Administrative Information Systems, departmental stakeholders, and project leadership, I used Power Automate to help solve publishing challenges and shape the platform’s organization, maintenance, and adoption.

The legacy environment

Legacy departmental resources included Human Resources, Institutional Effectiveness, Financial Aid, Advising, Veterans Benefits, Teaching and Learning Center, Curriculum and Assessment, Foundation, Admissions and Registration, Health Careers, and Adjunct Faculty Resources.

Their libraries, FAQs, announcements, forms, and operational information were valuable, but users navigated separate sites with different layouts and ownership practices.

The challenge was preserving institutional knowledge while modernizing the user experience and creating a more sustainable ownership model.

Decision point

We evaluated which platform should own each part of the experience.

Option 1: Recreate the legacy portal

This would preserve familiarity, but it risked carrying forward the same ownership, navigation, and maintenance problems into a new environment.

Option 2: Move everything into Experience

This would simplify the dashboard layer, but Ellucian Experience was not designed to replace deep departmental information repositories.

Option 3: Build a hybrid architecture

The final model used Ellucian Experience for portal access, SharePoint Online for departmental information, and supporting workflows for institutional communications.

The architecture

Ellucian Experience provides portal access, applications, student services, personalized resources, communications, and card-based discovery. SharePoint Online holds employee department resources, policies, forms, procedures, contacts, and internal documentation.

Power Automate, FTP delivery, and static publishing connect institutional communications to Ellucian Experience while preserving existing publishing needs.

SharePoint Online layer

  • Department information sites
  • Employee resources
  • Lists and libraries
  • Policies, procedures, and forms
  • Content ownership and governance

Ellucian Experience layer

  • Card-based portal experience
  • Student services and applications
  • Employee application access
  • Audience-specific resources
  • Centralized discovery and feedback

Key decision: separate student and employee experiences

We evaluated whether the student-facing resources from the legacy portal should also move into SharePoint Online.

Testing identified permission and governance challenges with broad student access in our implementation. Keeping student services in Experience and employee information in SharePoint Online gave each audience a clearer, more manageable path.

Experience delivers audience-specific cards, application integrations, student services, and institutional resources. SharePoint information sites support employee departmental knowledge and operational content.

The MyKCC Info Sites framework

I created the MyKCC Info Sites framework to connect employee information with the portal.

Employees needed a clear route to departmental procedures, forms, policies, contacts, and institutional knowledge.

The MyKCC Info Sites card connects department-owned SharePoint sites to Experience. Departments retain content ownership, and employees gain a central discovery point.

The MyKCC Info Sites card became one of the primary bridges between the legacy portal model and the modern digital workplace environment.

Content review and migration

Content required manual reconstruction using exports, command-line extraction, department interviews, content analysis, stakeholder review, and restructuring.

Departments helped decide which resources to migrate, rebuild, consolidate, or retire.

Financial Aid and Veterans Benefits sites largely duplicated information on kcc.edu or directed users back to it. We retired those duplicate repositories and linked to the authoritative public content.

Content review and consolidation gave departments a more sustainable set of resources to maintain.

Solving the Update publishing problem

Preserving the Update newsletter required a custom publishing workflow.

SharePoint Online’s RSS, public access, image handling, and distribution constraints complicated delivery through Experience.

With ITS and internal development resources, I helped design a workflow connecting SharePoint authoring, Power Automate processing, FTP delivery, static publishing, and Experience consumption.

Authoring

Update authors used a SharePoint Online editor environment with structured lists and libraries.

Workflow

Power Automate processed content and moved generated files through FTP-based publishing workflows.

Distribution

Static publishing infrastructure exposed content in a format the portal and archive systems could consume.

Launch, adoption, and training

The rollout included previews, staff reviews, card inventories, launch and student communications, contributor training, and feedback mechanisms.

Institution-specific documentation covered sites, lists, libraries, permissions, ownership, Update workflows, contributor onboarding, and portal navigation.

The Update workflow supported 22 unique article authors. Stakeholder reviews, ongoing contributor onboarding, and launch analytics helped validate adoption; information resources and governance continued to develop after launch.

Results

The replacement preserved institutional communications and established clearer ownership and publishing workflows. It also provides a foundation for continued Microsoft 365 improvements.

  • Built and organized approximately 16 SharePoint Online information sites
  • Created the MyKCC Info Sites framework for employee information resources
  • Supported more than 20 Update contributors across the institution
  • Reduced duplicate content repositories through rationalization and consolidation
  • Preserved institutional knowledge while modernizing delivery mechanisms
  • Continued platform growth, governance, and enhancement after launch

Adoption metrics

May–August 2023

MyKCC recorded 63,519 sessions, 43,064 views, 2,581 active users, 793,737 tracked events, and a 57.02% engagement rate.

January–June 2026

MyKCC recorded 125,049 sessions, 334,211 views, 4,017 active users, 1,796,787 tracked events, and a 70.05% engagement rate.

The platform continues to serve as a primary institutional resource several years after implementation.

Looking ahead

Ongoing work focuses on information-site governance, portal content, analytics, contributor support, and discovery across MyKCC, SharePoint Online, and the public website.
