Institutional Accessibility Program Mobilization

Rapid development of an institution-wide accessibility governance program aligned with DOJ Title II requirements and WCAG 2.1 AA readiness, establishing sustainable workflows, risk-based prioritization, and system-level improvements across a decentralized higher-ed digital ecosystem.

Kankakee Community College
June 2025 - Present

Overview

Led the rapid mobilization of an institutional accessibility program to support federal compliance readiness under a fixed deadline. Established governance structures, intake workflows, vendor review practices, and a risk-based remediation model across a decentralized web and content ecosystem.

Key Focus Areas

Governance
Established cross-divisional coordination, executive alignment, intake processes, and a defensible accessibility posture grounded in ownership and documentation.
Risk-Based Prioritization
Implemented a risk × usage model to focus remediation efforts on high-impact content, public-facing pages, student-facing systems, and legacy documents.
System Design
Improved CMS templates, form standards, content structures, and publishing practices to reduce future accessibility debt and support repeatable accessible patterns.
Institutional Adoption
Supported training, documentation, intake workflows, vendor governance, and shared practices to help accessibility work scale beyond isolated remediation.

This work established a sustainable, institution-wide approach to accessibility grounded in governance, prioritization, documentation, and continuous improvement rather than one-time remediation.
