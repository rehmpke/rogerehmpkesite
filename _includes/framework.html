How I Work

A practical framework for making web systems easier to govern

The work is not just redesign. It is a repeatable operating model for aligning content, accessibility, CMS workflows, analytics, vendors, and institutional accountability.

01

Assess

Inventory content, systems, templates, third-party tools, accessibility risk, analytics signals, and ownership gaps.

02

Prioritize

Use risk, usage, visibility, legal exposure, and user impact to decide what matters first.

03

Govern

Define intake, standards, review paths, documentation, remediation workflows, and vendor evidence.

04

Measure

Connect GA4, Search Console, accessibility findings, content performance, and governance outcomes.
