My work spans web strategy, UX, accessibility, CMS architecture, analytics, and content systems. It is centered on making complex digital environments easier to understand, operate, and trust.

As Director of Web Strategy and Digital Experience at Kankakee Community College, I oversee public-facing web strategy, accessibility governance, CMS architecture, analytics strategy, content standards, and AI-readiness initiatives. My role is to help those pieces work together so the public experience is clearer and the internal operation is more resilient.

Across roles — from web designer and developer to accessibility advocate, CMS architect, mentor, and digital strategy leader — I've guided redesigns, platform transitions, governance improvements, and accessibility compliance work. I tend to look for the path that solves the problem without creating a system that's difficult to sustain.
