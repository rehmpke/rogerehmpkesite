About

Digital strategy between institutional need and technical reality

My work sits between institutional strategy and technical execution: making public-facing digital systems clearer, more accessible, more governable, and easier for people to maintain.

I'm a higher-education web and digital strategy leader with more than 25 years of experience in UX, accessibility, CMS architecture, analytics, and content systems. My work focuses on turning complex web environments into clearer, more accessible, and more governable systems.

As Director of Web Strategy and Digital Experience at Kankakee Community College, I oversee public-facing web strategy, accessibility governance, CMS architecture, analytics strategy, content standards, and AI-readiness initiatives. My role helps digital systems serve users, reduce risk, and remain manageable over time.

Across roles — from web designer and developer to accessibility advocate, CMS architect, mentor, and digital strategy leader — I've guided redesigns, platform transitions, governance improvements, and accessibility compliance work. I prioritize practical systems that people can actually maintain.

Leadership Approach

Strategy that survives contact with institutional reality

Higher-ed web systems are rarely one system. They are content, vendors, PDFs, forms, CMS workflows, analytics, accessibility expectations, privacy concerns, and public trust signals moving at once.

  • Strategic ClarityAlign web systems with institutional goals, accessibility requirements, user needs, and long-term governance.
  • Technical FluencyConnect CMS architecture, front-end development, structured content, analytics, and accessibility into practical systems.
  • Collaborative LeadershipHelp marketing, ITS, academics, vendors, and leadership make better digital decisions together.
  • AI-Ready GovernanceStructure content, metadata, IA, and workflows so institutional information remains accurate and discoverable.
