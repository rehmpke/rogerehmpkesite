About

I'm a higher-education web and digital strategy leader with more than 25 years of experience in UX, accessibility, and content systems. My work focuses on creating clarity within complex digital environments — aligning user experience, institutional strategy, and long-term sustainability.

As Director of Web Strategy and Digital Experience at Kankakee Community College, I oversee the college’s digital ecosystem, including accessibility governance, CMS architecture, analytics strategy, and AI-readiness initiatives. My role centers on shaping web infrastructure and governance frameworks that support people, protect privacy, and strengthen organizational integrity.

Across roles — from CMS architect and accessibility advocate to mentor and systems designer — I have guided teams through major redesigns, governance transformations, and accessibility compliance initiatives. My approach prioritizes clarity, ethics, and long-term maintainability over short-term technical trends.

I also collaborate with peers across higher education and nonprofit sectors on emerging challenges including ethical AI adoption, summarization accuracy, digital equity, and sustainable governance frameworks that evolve alongside technology.

Leadership Approach:

  • Strategic Clarity — I connect web infrastructure with organizational goals, compliance requirements, and long-term sustainability.
  • Technical Fluency — From front-end architecture to CMS governance, I design scalable, accessible, and maintainable systems.
  • Collaborative Leadership — I guide cross-functional teams through digital transformations grounded in accessibility and purpose.
