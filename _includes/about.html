About

I'm a higher-education web and digital strategy leader with more than 25 years of experience in UX, accessibility, CMS architecture, analytics, and content systems. My work focuses on turning complex web environments into clearer, more accessible, and more governable systems.

As Director of Web Strategy and Digital Experience at Kankakee Community College, I oversee public-facing web strategy, accessibility governance, CMS architecture, analytics strategy, content standards, and AI-readiness initiatives. My role sits between institutional strategy and technical execution: helping digital systems serve users, reduce risk, and remain manageable over time.

Across roles — from web designer and developer to accessibility advocate, CMS architect, mentor, and digital strategy leader — I've guided redesigns, platform transitions, governance improvements, and accessibility compliance work. I prioritize practical systems that people can actually maintain.

I also work on emerging challenges in higher education, including responsible AI adoption, AI-readable content, summarization accuracy, digital accessibility, privacy alignment, and sustainable governance models.

Leadership Approach

Strong digital ecosystems depend on clear ownership, accessible systems, usable content, and technology choices that can be maintained over time. My work focuses on connecting people, policy, platforms, and public-facing experience.

  • Strategic Clarity — Aligning web systems with institutional goals, accessibility requirements, user needs, and long-term governance.
  • Technical Fluency — Connecting CMS architecture, front-end development, structured content, analytics, and accessibility into practical operating systems.
  • Collaborative Leadership — Helping teams make better digital decisions across marketing, ITS, academics, vendors, and institutional leadership.
  • AI-Informed Governance — Structuring content, metadata, IA, and workflows so institutional information remains accurate, discoverable, and trustworthy as AI changes search and content discovery.
